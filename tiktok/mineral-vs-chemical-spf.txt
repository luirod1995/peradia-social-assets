Mineral vs. chemical SPF — which one is right for you?

Mineral (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) sits on top of skin and reflects UV; it's a favorite for sensitive and reactive skin. Chemical filters absorb into the skin and tend to feel lighter and more invisible.

The best sunscreen is the one you'll actually wear every day. Both protect — pick the finish you love.
